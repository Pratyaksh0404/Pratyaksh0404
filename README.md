## About

I build ML systems that work in production — not just in notebooks.

At **NovTek Consulting** I shipped a real-time CCTV analytics platform running on client hardware: a computer vision pipeline that detects 14+ threat types (weapons, fire, behavioral violations, fight detection, camera tampering), sends automated WhatsApp and email alerts with burst snapshots, and generates shift compliance reports — all without cloud dependency. That is the kind of system I enjoy building.

Outside work I research **multi-agent reinforcement learning for 5G wireless networks** — a MAPPO + RIS + UAV swarm optimization framework that achieves 7–12% energy-efficiency gain over conventional baselines, implementing a published IEEE IoT Journal paper. I have also shipped a bioinformatics VAE for DNA anomaly detection (~98% AUC-ROC, live on HuggingFace) and an actively developed full-stack nutrition AI with local LLM inference.

**LeetCode Guardian — Rating 2219 (top ~1% globally).** 1000+ problems solved.

Currently open to **remote ML / Data Science roles and freelance computer vision or NLP projects.**
